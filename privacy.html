Privacy policy

Last updated 20 October 2026

What this site is

CIA AI Detector is an AI detection tool for use in YepaInvictus's Central Intelligence Agency on Roblox. It checks recruitment application answers for patterns common in AI-written text. It is not affiliated with the U.S. Central Intelligence Agency.

Google sign-in and your Google data

If you choose to sign in with Google, the site asks for one permission: access to the specific Google Drive files you pick with Google's file picker (the drive.file permission). It cannot see any other files in your Drive.

The site only reads the response spreadsheet you pick. It never edits, creates, deletes or shares any file. If your Google account does not have access to a spreadsheet, the site cannot read it.

Where your data goes

Everything runs in your own browser. Application responses, Discord messages you paste in, and files you load are processed on your device only. They are not sent to, or stored on, any server run by this site, and they are not shared with anyone. They are cleared when you close the page.

Your browser saves a few settings on your device so you don't have to re-enter them: your light or dark mode choice and which spreadsheet you picked. You can clear them at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser.

Third parties

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Fonts load from Google Fonts. The page loads Google's sign-in and Picker scripts, and signing in and picking a file use those Google services, under Google's privacy policy. The site uses no analytics, ads or tracking.

Removing access

You can remove the site's access to your Google account at any time from your Google account's third-party connections page.

Contact

Questions about this policy can go to the group's Headquarters leadership on Discord.
